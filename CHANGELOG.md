# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Initial release

Control your Philips Hue lights from your Pebble, talking to the local Hue
Bridge over WiFi (no cloud).

- **Rooms** — browse all rooms with *All Off*; long-press to toggle a room
  without drilling in.
- **Room control** — Up/Down to ramp brightness, Select to toggle, hold Select
  for scenes.
- **Scenes** — pick a scene and it applies instantly.
- Optimistic UI: the watch reacts immediately, then resyncs from the bridge.
- Link-button pairing with auto bridge discovery (or set the IP manually).

Requires the Pebble app on a phone sharing the bridge's WiFi. Built for Pebble
Time 2 (color); basalt and chalk also targeted.
